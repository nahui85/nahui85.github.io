Integration · Fintech / Tax

E-invoicing middleware for Costa Rica

An integration service connecting Odoo Online Enterprise with the Ministry of Finance (Hacienda) electronic receipts API: XML v4.4 generation, digital signing, consecutive numbering, submission, status tracking and write-back into Odoo.

Designed as independent services on Google Cloud Run with separate ingress and worker roles, structured logging and least-privilege containers.
